Our Belief

AI should reduce the mental load on families, not add to it

The world is changing fast. AI is reshaping how we work, learn, and communicate — but most AI tools are built for individuals, at work. Families are left to figure it out on their own.

We believe every household deserves an intelligent layer that handles the coordination overhead, anticipates needs before they become problems, and grows smarter the longer your family uses it — all while keeping your data private and your autonomy intact.

01

Household-aware, not just user-aware

Our AI understands the whole family — different schedules, ages, preferences, and roles — not just the person holding the phone.

02

Proactive, not reactive

It notices when soccer practice conflicts with a dinner reservation before you do. It suggests the grocery reorder before you run out. It plans so you don't have to.

03

Private by design

Your family's data trains your family's AI — not ours. No ads, no data brokering, no model training on your household's life.

04

AI that earns trust gradually

We start with suggestions, not automation. Your family stays in control — the AI earns more autonomy as you get comfortable with it.
