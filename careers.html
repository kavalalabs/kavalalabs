FAQ

Questions you're probably asking

Wait, is this paid?

Not in cash, not yet. You get equity, a premium AI plan, a MacBook that's yours to keep, bonus credits, surprise Apple gifts, and a founding seat. As the Labs grows and starts earning, we'll revisit cash compensation together.

How much equity are we talking?

A good amount, and we're upfront about it once we've talked. Exact numbers depend on the role and are shared 1:1 after the vibe check.

Can I do this alongside my full-time job?

That's the whole idea behind 10 hours a week. Just double-check your employer's side-gig or moonlighting policy first.

Why is 10 hrs/week "mandatory"?

Because a small crew only works if everyone actually shows up. Ten focused hours a week, consistently, beats a burst of 40 followed by ghosting. We'd rather be honest about it up front.

Where do I need to be?

Anywhere in India. The role is fully remote. Our founder is based in the US, so expect a regular sync call at a time that works for both India and the US.

What is Kavala Labs building, again?

AI-native products that help families run their lives: coordination, memories, household ops, kids' routines, and family finance. The full story is on our homepage.
